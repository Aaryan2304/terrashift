## 3. Radiometric conversion

Formula (ESA, PB 04.00 and later):

    reflectance = (DN + RADIO_ADD_OFFSET) / QUANTIFICATION_VALUE
    DN = 0 is NO_DATA

Read from the product metadata:

- QUANTIFICATION_VALUE = 10000
- RADIO_ADD_OFFSET = -1000 for all 13 bands in all four products

## 4. Water fraction and thresholds

NDWI (McFeeters) = (B03 - B08) / (B03 + B08), computed at 100 m.

| Product | NDWI > -0.2 | NDWI > -0.1 | NDWI > 0.0 | NDWI > 0.1 | NDWI > 0.2 |
|---------|-------------|-------------|------------|------------|------------|
| QBB_2022_12_23 | 75.93% | 67.64% | 62.28% | 61.35% | 61.08% |
| QBB_2023_05_12 | 75.34% | 63.00% | 60.72% | 59.21% | 32.33% |
| RGM_2023_10_06 | 9.73% | 2.18% | 0.36% | 0.15% | 0.02% |
| RGM_2026_10_22 | 39.86% | 6.22% | 0.49% | 0.20% | 0.07% |

Bands are resampled to the 100 m grid with bilinear interpolation. The
resampling method changes the fraction by about one percentage point;
nearest-neighbour resampling gives 63.10% and 61.01% for Pair A.
The threshold 0.0 is the physical boundary of the McFeeters index and is
the most stable across dates. The threshold 0.2 is not stable.
